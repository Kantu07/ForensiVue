DVR/NVR VENDOR RESEARCH

Problem:
Different DVR/NVR manufacturers may use different proprietary
storage formats and methods for exporting surveillance evidence.
This makes standardized forensic acquisition and analysis difficult.

Vendors identified in the problem statement:
1. Hikvision
2. Dahua
3. CP Plus
4. Honeywell
5. TP-Link
6. Godrej
7. Uniview
8. Matrix

For the 2-day prototype:
The team will demonstrate the forensic workflow using
standard video evidence files rather than implementing
complete proprietary filesystem recovery for every vendor.

Core workflow:
1. Evidence acquisition
2. Evidence identification
3. Metadata extraction
4. SHA-256 hashing
5. Integrity verification
6. Video analysis
7. Standardized forensic report

Important forensic principle:
The original evidence should be preserved and a working copy
should be used for examination. Hashing can be used to verify
that evidence has not been altered.
