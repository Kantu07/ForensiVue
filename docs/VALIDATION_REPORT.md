# Validation Report

**Tool:** ForensiVue v1.0 | **Date:** 2026-10-04

## 1. Scope and honesty statement

Validation was performed on **six synthetic disk images** generated by this project (`forensivue.testing.generate_suite`). The layouts are assumed, not real vendor formats. Results demonstrate that the software logic is correct against known ground truth. They do **not** demonstrate performance on real Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview or other devices.

**Definitions**
- *Exact:* recovered bytes equal the ground-truth SHA-256 of the original recording.
- *Combined exact:* matched by either index recovery or carving.
- *Damaged-recovered:* recovered but corrupted; never counted as intact. Ground truth stores the original pre-corruption hash.
- *Decodable:* clips that ffprobe can decode (includes non-matching clips).
- *Spurious:* outputs matching no ground-truth recording.
- *Rate:* Combined exact / Expected, capped at 100%.

## 5. Findings

1. **Contiguous and deleted recordings** (normal, deleted, multi_channel, mixed) are recovered exactly by index recovery.
2. **Fragmented recordings:** index recovery fails (it reads the first fragment plus gap), while carving reconstructs the exact original. This is the main demonstration of carving value.
3. **Damaged recordings** are recovered with corruption and flagged; the tool does not claim an intact recovery (0.0%).
4. **Tamper detection:** the audit logger tests confirm that modifying an entry breaks chain verification. Tamper detection through the dashboard shield: *record result here after manual test*.
5. **Hash verification:** acquisition tests confirm a modified image fails verification.

## 6. Limitations

- Synthetic images and assumed layouts only.
- Signatures are placeholders; vendor identification on real disks is untested.
- Carving can output spurious clips and decodable-but-incorrect clips; examiners must validate.
- Carving cannot separate interleaved channels.
- Timeline times in test images are fixed synthetic values (2020-09-13).
- Timestamp drift detection is exercised only where the synthetic images provide both times.
- AI analytics were not implemented.
- **Analysis time versus a manual workflow was not measured**, so no time-saving claim is made.

## 7. Conclusion

On synthetic data, the platform correctly acquires and hashes images, parses assumed layouts, recovers deleted and fragmented footage with exact-hash verification, reports damage honestly, and detects log tampering. Real-device validation is the required next step before operational use.
